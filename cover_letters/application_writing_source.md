## Professional background

### KEPCO E&C — 2014 onward

In 2014, Yonghyeon began his career at KEPCO E&C in the Nuclear Steam Supply System (NSSS) Development Division. As an engineer on the Technical Operations Team, he developed and maintained reactor design software and contributed to the cybersecurity design and protection of critical infrastructure in the nuclear industry.

This work showed him the importance of securing systems essential to national security and public welfare and helped establish his long-term commitment to critical-infrastructure cybersecurity.

### Ministry of Science and ICT, South Korea — 2018 onward

In 2018, Yonghyeon moved to the Ministry of Science and ICT as an Administrative Officer in the Cyber Security and Threat Management Division. He led and coordinated national responses to cyberattacks targeting critical infrastructure, including power grids, nuclear plants, and transportation systems.

His work included incident-response coordination, vulnerability assessment, information-security strategy, and development of approaches to mitigate emerging cyber threats. The role required balancing strong security protections with limited resources and the complexity of large infrastructure systems.

### Critical-infrastructure research — 2023

In 2023, Yonghyeon initiated a research project on cyber incident response for critical infrastructure in collaboration with the Korea Internet & Security Agency (KISA). The work analyzed hacking infrastructure used by international groups and explored how AI and data analytics could help predict and mitigate cyberattacks.

He also conducted vulnerability analyses of national infrastructure systems, including power-trading platforms, nuclear power plants, and railway systems. These experiences strengthened both his practical and theoretical foundation in cybersecurity.
